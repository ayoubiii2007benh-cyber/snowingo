Snowingo privacy policy

Snowingo – Multilingual Word Notebook (browser extension). Effective 17 November 2026 (version 2.18).

In short

Snowingo has no account and no server. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. Your words, review history and settings are stored only in your browser. A few features send the words or sentences you choose to the third-party services listed below, so they can be translated or explained.

What is stored, and where

  • In your browser (extension storage on your computer): your words, translations, notes, tags, review history, statistics, phrasebook, Focus Lock settings, subtitle settings, automatic backups, and your Google Gemini API key if you add one.
  • Synced by your browser: only your appearance choices (light/dark mode, theme, animation setting). They go through your browser's own sync, if you have it on, so they follow you to your other computers.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of this. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy. Generated captions run entirely on your computer: video audio is never sent anywhere and is not recorded.

Access to websites

The extension runs on the pages you visit so that you can double-click a word to save it, see learning subtitles in video players, open PDFs in its reader, and use Focus Lock if you switch it on. If you allow access to file URLs, it can also open PDFs stored on your computer; they are read only to display them, inside your browser. It does not read, record or transmit your browsing history or page contents. The only exception is the word or sentence you choose to translate, as described above.

Notifications

The daily review reminder is off by default. The browser asks for your permission only if you switch it on.

Children

Snowingo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
